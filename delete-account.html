Delete your Bennies account

This page provides the external account-deletion route for Bennies.

What deletion removes

  • Your Bennies authentication account and email address.
  • Your username and display name.
  • Friendships, requests, invitations and active multiplayer games.
  • Your own cloud Computer and Challenge statistics.
  • Account-linked block/report records.

Completed multiplayer results may remain in another player's historical statistics, but your identity is anonymised so that your former username/display name is not retained in that result.

Verify your account

Enter the email address associated with the Bennies account you want to delete. We will send a secure sign-in link to that address. This works whether you normally sign in with email and password, Google, or Apple.

Permanent deletion

This cannot be undone. Type DELETE below to enable permanent deletion.

Prefer to delete in the app?

Open Bennies → Help & Settings → Account → Delete Bennies Account.
