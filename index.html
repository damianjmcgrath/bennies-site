HOW IT WORKS

Easy to pick up.
Hard to put down.

Build sets, use the Bennie as a wildcard and empty your hand before your opponents do. Every round brings a new Bennie — and a new decision.

01 / DRAW↗

Pick your card

Start each turn by drawing from the deck or the discard pile.

02 / PLAY♠

Make your move

Lay down runs or matching cards, build on existing sets and put wild Bennies to work.

03 / WIN✦

Keep it low

Discard to finish your turn. The first player out ends the round, and the lowest score after 13 rounds wins.
